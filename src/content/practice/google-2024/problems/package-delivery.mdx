You are given an `m x n` map `grid` as a list of strings:

- `'W'` is the warehouse (there is exactly one),
- `'P'` is a packing facility holding one package,
- `'.'` is open ground, and
- `'#'` is a wall.

You start at the warehouse and must bring every package to it. You can carry **only one package at a time**: walk to a facility, pick up its package, and walk back to the warehouse. Each move goes to a horizontally or vertically adjacent cell that isn't a wall and costs `1`. You may walk through facilities and the warehouse.

Return the minimum total distance walked to deliver all packages, or `-1` if some facility can't be reached.

<Examples />

**Constraints:**

- `1 <= m, n <= 300`
- `grid[i]` has length `n` and consists of `'W'`, `'P'`, `'.'` and `'#'`.
- There is exactly one `'W'` and at least one `'P'`.

**Follow-up (from the interview):** you can now carry up to `k` packages at once, and pick them up from different facilities on the same trip. How would you approach it?
