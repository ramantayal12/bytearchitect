A group of people stood in a queue, and then got shuffled. Each person remembers two numbers, `people[i] = [h, k]`:

- `h` is their height, and
- `k` is how many people in front of them in the original queue were **at least as tall** (height `>= h`).

Reconstruct the original queue from `people`, and return it as a list in queue order, front first.

The input always comes from a real queue, so exactly one answer exists.

<Examples />

**Constraints:**

- `1 <= people.length <= 2000`
- `0 <= h <= 10^6`
- `0 <= k < people.length`
- `people` describes a valid queue.

**Follow-up:** can you avoid the `O(n²)` cost of inserting into the middle of an array?
